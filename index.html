RIGHT NOW

What is my job?

Where does it say that? · Interesting ≠ relevant · STOP at the period.

  1. JOB — Name the stem task before opening A–E.
  2. GIVEN — Facts only. No improvements.
  3. CONCLUSION — What is the argument trying to prove?
  4. GAP — Where is the evidence → conclusion link weak?
  5. TARGET — What must the credited answer do?
  6. Prove — Eliminate traps. Explain why the credited answer works.

If stuck

  • Flag and return.
  • Re-state JOB in one phrase.
  • Ask: does this do the TARGET?
